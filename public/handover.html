3

At the door

  1. The customer says they just want a call back.
  2. Show the QR code from the app. Turn the screen brightness up.
  3. They point their phone camera at it. No app needed.
  4. They fill in name, mobile, suburb and email, then tap Call me back.
  5. They see Thanks, you're all set. The lead is in.

No signal at the door? The page keeps their details on their phone and sends them once it's back online.

For the app team

Build the QR from the lead capture link above. For rep tracking, generate it from the rep link with the logged in rep's code. Use high error correction if you put a logo in the middle.

6

For the tech team: Insight Tracker

  1. Make an endpoint in Insight Tracker that accepts a JSON POST and creates a SALES LEAD task.
  2. Set its address as TRACKER_WEBHOOK_URL on the server, plus TRACKER_WEBHOOK_SECRET if you want one. The secret arrives in the X-Insight-Secret header.
  3. Return any 2xx to accept the lead. We wait up to 8 seconds.

One POST per lead:

{
  "id": "6f1c0e8a-3b1f-4f7e-9d0a-2c5b8e1a7d44",
  "at": "2026-11-10T08:30:00.000Z",
  "type": "callback",
  "consent": "callback-v1",
  "name": "Sam Citizen",
  "phone": "0412 345 678",
  "email": "sam@example.com",
  "suburb": "Parramatta",
  "pests": ["termites", "spiders"],
  "source": "qr",
  "rep": ""
}

Use id to drop duplicates. at is UTC. pests can include termites, rats-mice, cockroaches, spiders, ants, wasps, silverfish, other. Every lead is saved before we send it, so nothing is lost if the endpoint is down. Until it's live, leads can be backfilled from the CSV export.
